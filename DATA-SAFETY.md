# Data safety and public-repository rules

This is a public repository. All examples and test artifacts must be synthetic.

Do not submit:

- Real client, owner, submitter, animal, herd, flock, population, accession,
  specimen, order, result, or report data.
- Production screenshots, exports, interface messages, database extracts, logs,
  credentials, tokens, connection details, or private URLs.
- Meeting links, passcodes, attendee email addresses, or private contact details.
- Vendor-confidential material or copyrighted standards content without
  redistribution authority.

Synthetic examples should be realistic enough to exercise the workflow while
being invented for this project. Reviewers may compare them privately with real
laboratory workflows but must not upload the underlying operational records.

If potentially sensitive material is submitted, do not repeat it in an issue or
pull request. Contact a repository maintainer so access and removal can be
handled appropriately.
