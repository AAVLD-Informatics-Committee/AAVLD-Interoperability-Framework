# Connecting Veterinary Diagnostic Laboratories

## Building the AAVLD Interoperability Framework

**Date/time:** Thursday, October 15, 2026, 8:00 AM-12:00 PM

**Status:** Working symposium outline; speakers, facilitators, and detailed
timing remain subject to committee confirmation.

## Proposed flow

1. Welcome: Why Veterinary Laboratories Need an Interoperability Framework
2. Defining Veterinary Diagnostic Laboratory Interoperability
3. Publishing Laboratory Tests and Services
4. From Test Request to Laboratory Accession
5. Returning Results, Corrections, and Status
6. Mapping, Terminology, Governance, Adoption, and Change
7. Consensus, Unresolved Questions, and Next Steps

## Intended outputs

- Agreed scope and priority use cases
- Catalog minimum-data recommendations
- Request, acknowledgement, and accession workflow recommendations
- Result, correction, and amendment recommendations
- FHIR and terminology alignment findings
- Recorded consensus, dissent, open questions, owners, and next steps
