# Symposium working questions

## Scope and use cases

- Which exchanges must the initial framework support?
- Which workflows are explicitly deferred?
- What must remain traceable across organizational boundaries?

## Catalog

- What is the minimum catalog dataset?
- Which attributes are required, recommended, or optional?
- How are offerings identified, versioned, retired, and mapped?

## Request through accession

- What information is required to accept and accession a request?
- Which acknowledgement, warning, rejection, duplicate, and cancellation states
  are required?
- How are original and local identifiers preserved?

## Results and corrections

- Which interim, final, corrected, amended, cancelled, and replaced states are
  required?
- How is authorization represented?
- How are referred or subcontracted results identified?

## FHIR and terminology

- Which concepts map directly, require profiling, or expose veterinary gaps?
- Which FHIR baseline best fits participating systems and available tooling?
- Which terminology sources are usable and redistributable?

## Governance and adoption

- Who controls identifiers, profiles, terminology, and releases?
- How are breaking changes, mappings, pilots, and adoption tiers managed?
